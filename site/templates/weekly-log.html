Weekly Log — 2026-2027

One file, all year. Five minutes each Sunday during the planning block (chapter 03, Move 5). Evidence column filled the same night — that habit is the whole portfolio.

Hints that keep this alive

  • "This week we" is past tense and factual: "read ch. 6–7; made timeline cards," not "worked on history."
  • Evidence = pointer to a file you actually saved in weeks/week-NN/, or "photo taken [date]," or "in Saxon grade book p. 14." A pointer is enough on Sunday night; save the actual file by Wednesday.
  • Miss a week? Write "week skipped: illness" and move on. Honest gaps read as real life; silent gaps read as records problems.
  • Log lines feed the portfolio: quarterly, paste whole weeks into the portfolio-summaries prompt (prompts/portfolio-summaries.md).
