# Prompt: Build a Year Plan

*Category: planning · Use: once per year, then as-needed revisions ·
Pairs with: chapter 02.*

## Prep (2 minutes, not optional)

You need five facts ready: kids + levels, subject list, curriculum anchors,
calendar reality, energy pattern. Chapter 02 explains why. Nothing that
identifies your kid by name — descriptors only (chapter 08).

## Paste this prompt

```text
You are helping me plan a homeschool year. I am the parent-teacher and I make
decisions; you draft structure and flag problems. My context:

KIDS AND LEVELS
- [child descriptor A: e.g., "9, reads at 3rd grade level, strong at visual
  things, resistant writer"]
- [child descriptor B]

SUBJECTS
- Required by our state plan: [subjects]
- Chosen by me: [subjects], using [curriculum anchors, e.g., "Saxon Math 5,
  library-based history units"]

CALENDAR REALITY
- Year runs [date] to [date], [N] school weeks. We take off [week list:
  holidays, travel]. Expect [known rough patches].
- My planning energy: I plan once a week on Sundays, [minutes] maximum.

YOUR TASK
1. Draft a week-by-week table for all [N] weeks: week number, focus topic per
   subject, one milestone or assessment week every 12 weeks, buffer weeks
   marked "[BUFFER]".
2. Mark the week ends where each unit finishes, so I can see shape.
3. List risks: which weeks look overloaded for a household that gets sick and
   has errands? Which units are front-loaded too hard?
4. Propose ONE alternative shape (different ordering) so I can compare rather
   than accept your first draft.

Rules: no invented facts, no named field trips that need booking, no
assumptions about our budget or religion. Ask me up to 3 clarifying questions
BEFORE drafting if anything above is genuinely ambiguous.
```

## What to change every time

- The bracketed context, obviously. Numbers in brackets get real numbers.
- "buffer weeks marked [BUFFER]" → adjust to `also mark [ASSESSMENT]` weeks
  if your state requires evaluations or testing (from your chapter-07 file).

## Reading the output

- If it returns "180 days, 4 quarters, evenly distributed," bounce it back:
  "different shape — put the history units where the holiday weeks give me
  momentum." Second passes are where the plan becomes yours.
- Copy the table into `year-plan.md` (template:
  [templates/year-plan.md](../templates/year-plan.md)). Edit by hand:
  reading pace, bad calendar weeks, unrealistic volume.
- Keep the risks list; you'll need it in December exactly when you don't
  remember it.
