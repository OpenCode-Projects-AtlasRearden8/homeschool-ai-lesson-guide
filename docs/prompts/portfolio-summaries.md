# Prompt: Portfolio and Log Summaries

*Category: records · Use: weekly log lines, monthly index updates, quarterly
review write-ups · Pairs with: chapter 06.*

## Privacy check first

This prompt uses your log — which is fine, because your log was written with
the chapter-08 rules in mind: descriptors, no kid names, no diagnoses, no
addresses. If your log contains those, redact before pasting into any cloud
model, or run this on a local model.

## Paste this prompt (weekly version)

```text
Here is this week's log for my homeschool:

[PASTE THIS WEEK'S LOG ROWS]

Write ONE sentence per kid, per subject, in portfolio style — warm to read,
specific about what was done, no adjectives about competence. Portfolio
language, not chat language. Nothing invented, nothing moralizing about
attitude or effort; if something is missing from the log, showcase nothing —
just ask me.
```

Paste the output straight into your portfolio index draft. You edit it until
it sounds like you; that's a two-minute job weekly instead of a two-hour job
in June.

## Paste this prompt (quarterly version)

```text
Here are 12 weeks of log entries:

[PASTE WEEKS' LOG ROWS]

Summarize this quarter:
1. Units COMPLETED (with weeks). Use my own log, nothing extrapolated.
2. Books read: titles only, I'll verify.
3. Subjects that lagged, and the pattern of when (date ranges).
4. Two sentences of honest narrative summary for an records evaluator:
   what got accomplished, what didn't, what next quarter adjusts.
5. The single most encouraging thing a tired teacher should hear about this
   quarter, based ONLY on evidence above, one sentence.
```

Item 5 exists because the review is for you, not just the paper trail —
quarterly reviews are when homeschool parents discover they did far more
than they remember.

## Keep the output, not the chat

Generated summaries go into `portfolio-index.md` / quarter notes in your
records folder (chapter 06's tree). The chat itself is deleted; the prose
lives with the rest of the year.
