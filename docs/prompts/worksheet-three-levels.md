# Prompt: Three Worksheets, One Lesson

*Category: materials · Use: after picking the weekly plan's winner · Pairs
with: chapter 04.*

## Prep (3 minutes)

You need the *lesson content* (your chosen lesson text from the weekly plan,
or the passage/topic itself) and your kids' level descriptors. Level
descriptors by band — check the chapter 04 table if unsure.

## Paste this prompt

```text
Here is one lesson, by me, for my household. Content:

[PASTE THE LESSON TEXT OR TOPIC + CORE FACTS — verified per chapter 05]

Make THREE worksheets from this same content. Same facts, same topic, three
reading bands:

Sheet 1 — EMERGING reader: 8-word-max sentences, one idea per line, 4
questions total, generous spacing, one drawing or circling task, NO paragraph
format.

Sheet 2 — ON LEVEL: 12-word-max sentences, 6 questions, mix of short answer,
one sequencing task, one "why" question.

Sheet 3 — ADVANCED: natural prose, 8 questions, at least two inferential
(compare/explain), one primary-source-style short quote for them to respond
to (quote must be real and I will verify it — if unsure, write [QUOTE:
describe the kind of quote needed] instead of inventing).

Also produce:
- An ANSWER KEY for all three.
- A three-line note: what changed between sheets, so I can see they're the
  same lesson.
- A check: confirm no sheet reveals phrasing that would be an answer on a
  harder sheet.

Do not simplify the FACTS between sheets — only the language and the
questions may differ. If any fact in my pasted content looks suspicious or
self-contradictory, list it at the top instead of silently using it.
```

## The three checks before printing

1. **Facts** — you verified before generating (chapter 05). If "sounds
   suspicious" flags appear, resolve them first.
2. **Same-lesson check** — read the three-line note: every level answers
   questions about the same content.
3. **Answer key sanity** — work two problems/key answers yourself. Thirty
   seconds; catches broken-key weeks (see chapter 10, item 7).

## Variations worth saving

- **12-year-old reluctant writer, same content:** change the 4-6 question
  output to "circle / match / draw / two words" and add "max 4 sentences of
  writing total."
- **Family co-op group, mixed levels at one table:** add "add a fourth
  sheet, non-competitive, for an adult volunteer helper with the same
  content, so they can lead small groups."
- **Everything oral day (low energy):** ask for "the same three levels as a
  Q&A script to read aloud, answers in parentheses."
